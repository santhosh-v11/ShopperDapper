# ShopperDapper

**Shop from your favourite Instagram vendors. No DMs. Just buy.**

---

## How it works?

ShopperDapper is a marketplace for men's accessories shoes, watches, wallets, bracelets and sunglasses built for Instagram sellers who are tired of answering the same DMs all day.

Every seller gets their own storefront. Every product gets its own link. The seller posts on Instagram like they always do, drops the link in the caption, and buyers can buy directly. No "price?" DMs. No waiting. No GPay to a stranger with zero proof.

---

## The problem

Anyone who buys from Instagram shops knows this pain.

You see a nice watch. You DM them. You wait 6 hours. They reply with the price. You ask about COD. They say no. You pay on UPI. The product shows up 3 weeks later in the wrong colour. They've stopped replying.

And from the seller's side ,they're running a 30,000-follower shoe page from their phone, replying to 100+ is not easy. They're losing sales they don't even know about.

The real problem isn't bad sellers or impatient buyers. It's that Instagram was never built for commerce. There's no price, no cart, no checkout, no order tracking. It's just photos and DMs.

**Meta made it worse** they removed Instagram's native checkout in September 2025. Sellers now have literally no in-app payment option.They are will redirect buyer to external source .

---

## The solution

ShopperDapper gives Instagram sellers a real storefront in under 20 minutes.

Sign up, connect your Instagram handle, add your products with photos and prices, and you get a link `shopperdapper.in/store/yourhandle`. Post it in your bio. Drop it in your captions. Share it in your Stories.

Buyers click the link, see your full catalogue, pick what they want, and pay with UPI, card, or COD via Razorpay. They get an order confirmation. They can track their delivery. And you get a dashboard with all your orders and earnings in one place.

That's it. The DM inbox goes from 150 messages a day to maybe 5.

## condition labels

Every product needs one of these four labels. This is non-negotiable and shows up on every listing.

| Label | What it means |
|-------|--------------|
| Brand New | Factory sealed, original packaging |
| First Copy | High-quality replica of a branded product |
| Thrift | Pre-owned, good condition |
| Refurbished | Pre-owned, cleaned and repaired |

This is what makes the platform trustworthy. No surprises for buyers. No hiding behind vague product descriptions.

---

## Roles

**Sellers** are Instagram and Facebook resellers  shoe flippers, watch sellers, thrift stores, importers. They create a store, list their products, and share links in their content.

**Buyers** are guys who already follow these sellers on Instagram and want to buy without the hassle. They can also search for a seller directly by their Instagram handle.

**Admin** (developer) approves new sellers before they go live, handles disputes, and keeps the platform clean.
